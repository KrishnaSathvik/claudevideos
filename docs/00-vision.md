# 00 · Vision and positioning

## The idea

Use AI coding agents (Claude Code) as a programmable film studio to make videos that stand out on YouTube, TikTok,
Instagram Reels and X. The agent researches, writes, directs, draws/animates, scores and mixes; the human chooses the
references, approves the look and checks the facts.

## Positioning: Opus is the camera, not the brand

- "I make cool animations with Claude" is already ordinary. After Opus 5.5 (22 Sep 2026) there was an explosion of
  Opus-made motion graphics; one index listed ~1,276 public Opus videos from ~1,007 creators (as reported in chat,
  claudevideo.org; not re-verified). Viral hits were mostly launch films, kinetic type and UI loops, **not** explainers.
- So the identity must be the **content and a recognisable visual language**, never the tool. Nobody subscribes to
  Pixar for its renderer.
- Viewers are tiring of "AI sameness": recurring hooks, AI voices, template visuals and end cards.

## Candidate directions (not locked)

| Direction | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Invisible Systems**: how everyday systems really work (card payments, a ChatGPT answer, a WhatsApp message, GPS, power grids) | Needs real research and sourced facts; little strong competition found for e.g. "tap to pay" explainers |
| Tutorials about making Claude animations | Crowding fast; tool-centric identity, weaker |
| Cinematic shorts, 3D transformations, surreal animation, miniature worlds, science explainers | To be decided from the owner's reference videos |

Original Invisible Systems ideas (kept for later): one visual universe where data is glowing particles, services are
physical places (API = doorway, database = vault, cache = drawer, queue = conveyor, payment network = railway, region =
city); episodes like "What happens after you tap your card?", "...after you ask ChatGPT a question?", "Where does your
WhatsApp message go?"; long videos built from independently rendered 40–60 s sequences; 3–5 Shorts per research
package. **Lesson from Episode 001:** a metaphor diagram is not a film; direction and reference come first.

## A house style, not a demo reel

Using a different style every episode looks like a demo reel, not a brand. Pick one recognisable visual language
(derived from references), keep it consistent, and evolve it deliberately.

## Quality and YouTube rules

- YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"** (15 Jul 2025): mass-produced, templated, low-value
  videos are demonetised. Using AI or a synthetic voice alone does not disqualify a channel; generic templated output does.
  (TechCrunch 2025-07-09; creatorhandbook.net)
- YouTube Partner Program full ad-revenue threshold (as checked in chat, Oct 2026): 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 valid
  public long-form watch hours in 12 months, or 10M valid Shorts views in 90 days.
- Bad: AI picks topic → AI script → same template → generic TTS → upload 5/day.
  Good: original question → researched story → verified claims → custom direction → original visuals → recognisable
  language → upload when genuinely good.
- Voice becomes part of the brand: eventually the owner's own narration, a consistent licensed voice, or a designed
  narrator. Kokoro/Piper TTS is for prototypes only.
- Every fact goes in `FACTS.json` with a source (fact → source → visual claim). Pin tool versions; upgrade deliberately.
